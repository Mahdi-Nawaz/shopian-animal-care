About the Initiative Concept

Built for Kashmir’s Terrain & Seasons.

In District Shopian, Jammu & Kashmir, high-altitude winters (Chillai Kalan), busy fruit-harvest transport along apple orchard roads, and steep stretches toward Mughal Road and Hirpora create urgent veterinary challenges for street dogs, livestock, and injured birds.

This UI/UX web concept demonstrates how local volunteers, neighborhood feeders, and veterinary responders in Shopian Town and Arhama can organize rapid road trauma triage, humane vaccination drives, and insulated winter shelters with complete cost transparency.

Rapid Road Trauma Triage

First-aid splinting & wound care on orchard & highway routes.

Anti-Rabies & ABC Drives

Humane population care & annual immunization across wards.

Winter Shelter & Nutrition

Reflective thermal coats, dry straw kennels & warm meals.
